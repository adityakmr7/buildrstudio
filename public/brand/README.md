# BuildrStudio / Numa brand concept

Open `index.html` through a local HTTP server or the existing app at `/brand/index.html`.

Design direction: a premium editorial company identity with a warm consumer product identity. Design variance 6, motion intensity 2, visual density 3. Native HTML/CSS; light theme aligned with the repository requirement. No service integrations or existing marketplace changes.

Company: BuildrStudio, an independent product company.
First product: Numa, a pregnancy and baby tracker and companion.

Tokens: evergreen #153E35, mint #D7E9DB, porcelain #F7F9F6, fern #536A62. Typography: Georgia for editorial display, self-hosted Geist for UI. 8px spacing rhythm, 8px button corners, 20px surface corners.

Marks are editable SVG concepts; wordmark lettering uses live type, not finalized custom lettering. Naming clearance, clinical sourcing/review, app development and Firebase setup remain future work.

Generated art: built-in image generation, prompt: "Create one premium brand art image for Numa, a pregnancy companion product of BuildrStudio. Landscape 3:2 composition. An original independent tiny companion creature, smooth mint porcelain pebble body with two small black eyes, subtle smile, short rounded arms and feet, a single sprout-like tuft. Not a baby, fetus, animal or doctor. Creature placed right of center beside two sculptural interlocking evergreen ceramic arches on a pale cool porcelain studio background. Sophisticated tactile 3D product photography, soft daylight, beautifully restrained mint and deep evergreen palette, fine soft shadows. No text, no logos, no gradients, no UI. Keep subject fully visible with generous negative space. This is a visual identity concept."
